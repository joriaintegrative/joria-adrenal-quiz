What's your stress pattern?
Vessel Edition
For cis women, trans men, and nonbinary people with ovaries, whether or not you're currently cycling, including anyone navigating fertility, hormone therapy, or a body that doesn't fit a textbook pattern.
This quiz will help you identify your stress pattern. Based on your responses, you will receive one of six patterns. Each pattern includes a description and questions to bring to your care team. 13 questions. Select every answer that applies. More than one is common. This is a starting point (or continuation) for noticing what your body may be telling you.
This quiz is for educational purposes only. It is not a clinical diagnosis. Please work with a licensed provider for individualized care.
