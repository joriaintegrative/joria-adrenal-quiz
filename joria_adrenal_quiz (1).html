What's your adrenal profile?
This quiz will help you identify your adrenal stress pattern. Based on your responses, you will receive one of six profiles. Each profile includes a description and questions to bring to your care team. This quiz is educational. It is not a clinical diagnosis. Use it as a starting point for self-understanding and provider conversation.
Question 1 of 13
1. How do you typically feel when you wake up in the morning?
2. How would you describe your energy across the day?
3. How is your sleep?
4. How often do you feel anxious or on edge?
5. What is your relationship to stress right now?
6. Do you experience spotting before your period begins, or a luteal phase shorter than 10 days?
Skip this question if you are not currently cycling or tracking your cycle.
7. How are your salt cravings?
8. How does your body respond to exercise right now?
9. How consistent are your symptoms day to day?
10. Do you experience symptoms of androgen excess like acne along the jawline or chin, or unexplained hair thinning on the scalp?
11. How would you describe your libido?
12. How do you feel in the 24 to 48 hours after a high-stress event or intense workout?
13. When you read about allostatic load, the idea that cumulative stress including systemic stress like racism, economic strain, or medical trauma has a measurable biological cost, did it resonate with your experience?
